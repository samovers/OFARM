# Material occupancy source evidence, draft v0.1

The primary trust boundary is **material occupancy source evidence**. This owner interface represents and compares selected occupancy source facts. It creates no occupancy, movement, durable identity, authority, or lifecycle rule. It is draft and non-default, and the checker is opt-in. The active source texts and identity lifecycle schema remain unchanged.

The Identity and Lifecycle RFC distinguishes a containment unit from the lot or material it contains and preserves container identity across occupancy episodes. Its continuity record is not a full occupancy record: the crate-44 example names the same container as old and new identity, without naming the occupant or a complete interval. This separately authorized interface fills that evidence gap. It does not reinterpret a continuity token as proof of occupancy.

## Records and complete selected inventory

Each new `ofarm.material-occupancy-evidence.v0.1` record has an episode identity and full facts. Direction is material to place, with a typed `LOT` or `MATERIAL` occupant and a typed `CONTAINER`, `STORAGE_LOCATION` or `FACILITY` endpoint. Occupant and place must have distinct logical identities, including when their revision selectors differ. Every endpoint and relation concept uses the complete immutable selector, either `{ref,digest}` or `{ref,revisionRef}`. The relation concept's selected revision is retained, with the explicit relationship `OCCUPIES`.

Start and end posture are explicit. A known boundary includes its timestamp and whether it is included. An unknown or unbounded boundary needs its exact source basis. An open end is only open as of the stated observation, with its basis; it is never infinity. Canonical UTC timestamps preserve up to nine fractional digits without rounding. Invalid calendar dates, offsets, leap seconds and hour 24 are rejected. Source applicability is a closed point or an interval with explicit boundary inclusion. Its interval must cover the independently selected query; each known occupancy endpoint independently constrains the source's own applicability. An unknown opposite endpoint cannot hide a contradiction in a known start/end or an open-end observation. Unknown endpoints are retained and cannot establish temporal coverage.

Occupied quantity keeps a decimal string, quantity-kind and unit identities, original lexical amount and unit, and qualifier. No conversion, arithmetic equivalence, normalization or dimensional inference is performed. An explicitly unreported amount keeps its unit identity and reason. The occupied cohort is either an exact immutable selection or explicitly unreported. Relevant extent is whole, an exact partial-extent binding, or explicitly unresolved. At least a reported quantity or identified cohort and resolved extent are needed for the report's conditional unqualified-claim indicator. This indicator never changes the retained source facts.

Source validity, dispute and revision posture are retained with their owner evidence and assessment time. Superseded records require their exact successor evidence; other revision postures must not claim successors. Invalid, disputed, superseded and unknown facts may be compared and reported for fidelity. They cannot support the conditional unqualified-claim indicator. Historical applicable sources are distinct from current sources and remain explicitly labelled. This interface does not make any of these assessments itself.

The independently selected owner context contains the query, full ordered episode inventory, all full immutable selectors, raw-byte hashes, value digests, digest modes and source roles. Its completeness declaration is an input from the source owner. The checker proves exact set/order agreement with that selection; it does not prove a live store was exhaustively queried. A complete empty inventory is represented by zero selected episodes, with the exact place, query, owner identity and selection basis still required. This is conditional evidence of the owner's selected empty set, not independently proven absence. Repeated and overlapping episodes retain separate immutable source identities and original intervals. Nothing deduplicates, collapses or sums episodes. A missing, extra, reordered or duplicate selected episode is rejected.

## Original bytes and independent selection

`verify(request_bytes, context_bytes, records, trusted_pins=..., package_root=..., source_root=...)` takes original bytes, never a generic admitted Boolean or opaque resolver callback. `records` maps `selector_key(full_binding)` to original bytes. The context, request and contract SHA-256 pins must be independently selected by the caller, not copied from the untrusted input being evaluated. That caller selection is an explicit trust assumption; this offline checker cannot authenticate it.

Every owned reference in the facts, context owner identity and selection basis must have exactly one source mapping. Extra or missing mappings fail. Supporting records retain their complete bytes. For `JCS_JSON`, the selected value digest is SHA-256 of RFC 8785 JSON; for `RAW_BYTES`, it is SHA-256 of the original bytes. A digest selector must equal that value digest. A revision selector is never translated into a digest selector: the independently selected mapping binds the complete revision selector to both hashes. No alias inference is permitted. Original serialization matters even when two JSON encodings have the same canonical value.

Record schemas are closed. Duplicate names, malformed UTF-8, BOMs, nonfinite numbers, unsafe canonical JSON values, oversized input and excessive nesting fail. There are no external schema fetches or network resolvers. The pinned contract covers the exact checker, three schemas and the original semantic source bytes. Supporting event, measurement, identity, unit, extent, source-validity and lifecycle records remain owned by their existing validators. This interface preserves all their bytes without validating or redefining those owners' semantics. It restricts neither event family nor measurement result kind.

The request lists the full expected facts for each selected episode. The checker compares every field, including direction, endpoint selectors, relation revision, timestamps, boundary inclusion, quantity kind, unit, cohort, extent, source evidence and status. A changed expected field cannot be hidden by an unchanged quantity number or episode label. A bare `IdentityLifecycleChange` is rejected as the full record; it may be retained as separately bound lifecycle evidence.

## Report and use

`MATCHED_SELECTED_OCCUPANCY_EVIDENCE` means conditional exact source comparison only. The report preserves every episode and all selected source mappings, with temporal coverage and whether selected posture supports an unqualified claim. Reports always say runtime admission and actual inventory completeness are `NOT_ESTABLISHED`. Error reports provide no partially accepted episode set. A matching disputed, superseded or unresolved record must not be treated as clean current occupancy merely because its bytes matched.

The consumer remains responsible for deriving its required source roles and full original-input manifest, selecting the actual owner context independently, calling the exact interface, checking the report's qualifications and comparing these complete facts with its allegation. It must preserve all applicable episodes. It may not replace this work with a consumer-supplied expected dictionary, optional callback or self-asserted admission. This profile neither proves source truth nor grants authority to promote an assertion.

Fictional fixtures demonstrate interface behavior. Their owner contexts and supporting illustrative records are assumptions, not authentic owner decisions or new foreign schemas. Tests cover both immutable selector modes, all endpoint kinds, overlap/repetition, boundary postures, quantity/cohort/extent variants, source posture and hostile substitutions. Existing event and measurement bytes are opaque supporting evidence and do not become occupancy authority.
