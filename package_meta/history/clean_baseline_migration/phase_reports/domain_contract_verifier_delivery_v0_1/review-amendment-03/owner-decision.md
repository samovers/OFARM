# Domain owner decision for amendment 03

Resolve both follow-ups within **AssertionRecord domain meaning and validation integrity**. The source is published head `1a2583d24aa7d280e257e2d54d1c7c3708b623b6`. This proposal changes candidate source fidelity and finite checks; it changes no authority, source-adoption rule, runtime, Event Ingress producer, CI or merge boundary.

The quantity schema's closed qualifier enum permits HUMAN_REPORTED and MACHINE_REPORTED alongside other quantity qualifications. Neither that schema nor the measurement schema defines LAB_RESULT/LAB_REPORT as automatically machine-reported. The quantity RFC requires retaining original reported values/units; the measurement RFC preserves censored results and their source basis. The exact pins and JSON pointers are in `source-pins-v4.json`. This decision therefore rejects automatic relabelling of the lab LOQ as MACHINE_REPORTED.

W05 retains HUMAN_REPORTED for both quantities, with explicit, separately authored fictional records explaining those roles. `owner-records-v4.json` contains a human transcription of the laboratory's LOQ scalar and a human-authored example criterion. It is not a real lab report, statutory rule, admitted owner response or claim of an existing producer. The criterion record fixes `LE`, threshold `1 mg/kg`, its own qualifier and lexical text before the builder reads the candidate claim. It is digest-pinned through the independent expectations. The build no longer copies the threshold from the claim to define its expected provenance.

The scalar LOQ text `2` and full censored-result text `< 2 mg/kg` describe different selected values. The former now comes from `/loq/originalLexicalValue` in the exact resolved transcription record. The latter remains the MeasurementEvidence result's original text and is not converted into a point result. A separate hypothetical r2 source using scalar text `2.00` passes when the claim retains it exactly; changing that claim text to `2` fails. Wrong qualifiers, unknown qualifiers and a different or missing reporting role also fail. The source role is checked separately from the numeric comparison and enum membership.

W05 adds required `operand.limit.provenance` and `threshold.provenance` objects with exact source bindings and value/lexical/qualifier pointers. Its new quantity-source binding is ordinary assertion evidence and has exact membership and typed citation checks. Both resolved record digests and selectors are explicitly required in the domain evidence closure; `source-resolution-trace-v4.json` illustrates that closure without claiming a real verifier trace. The limit remains `2 mg/kg`, the comparison remains `<= 1 mg/kg`, and the conclusion remains INDETERMINATE. Neither qualifier changes. Twenty-four whole examples are entirely unchanged; W05's added source meaning is exactly re-rendered and hashed. All previous bytes remain historical inputs.

It checks measured sources in C03/C04/C09 and nested C06, rate sources, required qualifications, and the new scalar source objects before dependent access. Binding objects are closed and require exact typed components; pointers require absolute RFC6901 syntax. Unknown members and inconsistent aliases fail. Strings, lists, numbers and booleans cannot pass or trigger the former alias-access exceptions. These remain bounded role checks, not a full proposed-body schema or complete domain verifier.
