## Exact projection and ownership

`projection-contract-v2.json` enumerates the projection members and branch rules now. Its exact rendering remains `"Pending claim: " + JCS(P) + "."`. The new examples place P at `claimContent` and keep fictional owner facts in the separate `governedContextExample`. Unknown projection members must fail; exclusions use exact roles and selectors, never a recursive name filter.

The deciding source is PR23 at `6c5c6eb754baecfa38b7e364fc3d0318ae1ad89f`, §§6.1–6.2. The selected authorization path supplies the result's `/assertedByPartyRef`; the intents have no reporter property. For online submission the trusted runtime supplies top-level `/assertedAt` before intent validation/hash. For offline submission the immutable act evidence supplies the timestamp and binds the actual Party/basis and exact submitted subject, scope, body and evidence. Its record must precede the complete intent digest.

Therefore the projection excludes `/assertedAt`, `/assertionActPosture`, `/assertionActEvidenceBinding` and the authorization-selected reporter for both postures. These exclusions do **not** remove any field from the full intent/result hash. `assertionBody.assertionPosture` INITIAL/CORRECTION, operation INTENDED/PERFORMED, alleged performer, every domain time/extent/qualifier, ordinary evidence, relationship context, event details and quantity/material distinctions remain rendered. A domain act's reporter or time is not removed merely because it has a similar name.

The finite offline order is **author's domain content → rendered body → independently authenticated act evidence over that body → evidence digest/binding → complete intent/hash → authorization/result and domain validation → separate trace**. Ordinary support cannot alias the self-act record or wrap it to recreate the cycle. The complete verification still resolves the act evidence, recomputes its digest, compares the exact body/content, timestamp and actual authority-selected Party/basis, and applies the existing admission policy. No changed PR23 authority or runtime-authored body is required.
